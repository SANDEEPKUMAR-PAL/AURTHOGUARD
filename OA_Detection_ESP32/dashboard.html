Leg & Sensor Calibration

How to Calibrate (For First-Time Users):
  1. Wear All 5 Sensors: Strap Thigh IMU (above knee), Shin IMU (below knee), Flex strip over the front kneecap, and Heel/Forefoot FSRs inside the shoe.
  2. Option 1 — Auto Calibrate (Recommended): Have the patient stand straight & still (0°) for 2 seconds, then click Auto Calibrate (Stand Straight 0°). The knee angle zeros immediately. (Optional: bend knee ~90° and click Step 2 to auto-check flex & strap polarity.)
  3. Option 2 — Manual Per-Sensor Tuning: Because leg thickness and shoe fit vary per person, adjust any individual sensor card below (lift foot slightly before clicking Heel/Forefoot Tare), then click Save & Sync All 5 Sensors.

Sensor Calibration & Telemetry Controls Explained

Term / Setting What It Means & When to Use It
FSR Tare (ADC)Resting shoe/sock squeeze subtracted from raw Heel/Forefoot FSR before % load math. Always lift the foot slightly off the ground before clicking Tare.
IMU Polarity (+1 / -1)Flips flexion sign if a sensor board was strapped upside-down or facing backwards, so bending your knee always increases positively (+0° to +135°).
Pitch / Roll Offset (°)Static strap mounting slope captured during standing straight (0°), removing leg-shape differences across patients.
0° Straight / 90° Bent ADCMaps the Knee Flex strip resistance range per knee girth (0° = 0.0 norm, 90° = 1.0 norm). Triggers stiffness alert if Flex Ratio < 0.25 past 45° bend.
OA Risk Tiers (0–100)Low (<35): Green LED ON. Moderate (35–65): Watch early markers. High (>65): Critical Red LED + Buzzer ON.
